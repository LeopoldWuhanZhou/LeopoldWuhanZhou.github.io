This is the 3+x seminar on The Integrability Problems in Mathematical Physics, organised by Xiaomeng Xu in 2025 Fall at Peking University.

Here I introduced the paper, Some Exact Results for the Many-Body Problem in one Dimension with Repulsive Delta-Function Interaction, by C.N. Yang in 1967, which is one of the first origins of the Yang-Baxter equation.
