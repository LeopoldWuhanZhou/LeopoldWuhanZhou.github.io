This is the 3+x seminar on The Integrability Problems in Mathematical Physics, organised by Xiaomeng Xu in 2025 Fall at Peking University.

The reference is GTM 155 Quantum Groups X6-X7, by Christian Kassel. I introduced the concept of braid groups $$B_n$$, some representations of $B_n$ and the relation with $$S_n$$.
